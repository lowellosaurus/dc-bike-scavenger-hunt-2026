Need to know

Questions,
answered.

Still wondering about something?

Do I need to be a serious cyclist?+

Not at all. The route is designed for casual city riders and you’ll move at your team’s pace. If you can comfortably ride about 12 miles with stops, you’re set.

What kind of bike should I bring?+

Any roadworthy bike works—including Capital Bikeshare. Check your tires and brakes beforehand, and don’t forget your helmet.

What if I don’t have a team?+

Register solo and choose the matchmaking option. We’ll introduce you to a friendly crew before event day.

Is this a race?+

Only if you want it to be. Points come from solved clues and creative challenges, not just speed. The best route is rarely the fastest one.

What happens if it rains?+

A little drizzle makes the story better. We’ll ride in light rain and announce any severe-weather changes by email the day before.
