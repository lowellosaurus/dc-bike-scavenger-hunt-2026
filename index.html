THE PREPARATION

Now (before September 7th)

  • Assemble a team of 2–4 sleuthing cyclists.
  • Text Lowell one clue for each person on your team.

THE SCAVENGER HUNT

Saturday, September 12th

  1. 11:45

    Meet at a central location in DC (to be announced later).

  2. 11:55

    Receive your clue sheet, map, and brief rules reminder.

  3. 12:00

    Go go go! Tear into your packet, start solving the clues, and set off to find them.

  4. 12–5

    Find clues, take pictures with or at them, and text them to Lowell for confirmation.

  5. 5:00

    Kickstands down! The team that found the most clues wins (to be announced later).
